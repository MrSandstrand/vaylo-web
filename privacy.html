Privacy

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you use the early-access form we store the details you type in: your role, name, email, ZIP code, company and any message, plus the date and the page you sent it from.

Why

To contact you about early access, answer your question, and follow up with plumbers, property managers, insurers, distributors and investors who ask to hear from us. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers on this site.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored with our database provider (Supabase, US region) and, if you send a message, forwarded by email to our team. Only the Vaylo team has access.

How long

Until launch and for up to 24 months after, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

Email hello@getvaylo.io to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop hearing from us. Every email we send also has a way to opt out.
